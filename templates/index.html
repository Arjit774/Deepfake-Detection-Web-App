Under the hood

Three stages, one verdict

01 · SAMPLING

Frames are extracted

OpenCV seeks evenly across the clip, centre-crops each frame to a square, and resizes it to 224×224 RGB. Images skip extraction and enter as a single frame.

02 · FEATURES

InceptionV3 encodes

Each frame becomes a 2048-dimensional feature vector. Up to 20 vectors form one sequence, and longer clips produce additional windows.

03 · VERDICT

The GRU decides

A stacked GRU reads the temporal sequence with a length mask, outputs a sigmoid fake probability, and the window scores are averaged.

Note: This is a machine-learning screening tool, not forensic proof. Predictions can be wrong, especially on compressed, low-light, or out-of-distribution media. Always combine automated results with human review before making a final judgment.
